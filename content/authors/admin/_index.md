My research interests focus on the theoretical foundations and methodological advances of causal inference, causal discovery, graphical models, and functional data analysis. In particular, I work on establishing new statistical frameworks that leverage the causal invariance assumption to achieve simultaneous identification of direct causes for a given target variable and causal effect estimation. I am broadly motivated by translating complex real-world problems into rigorous theoretical advances.
